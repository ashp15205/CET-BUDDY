# 🏫 CET Buddy : MHT-CET College Predictor

**CET Buddy** is a lightning-fast, beautifully designed web tool that helps MHT-CET engineering aspirants instantly find eligible colleges in Maharashtra based on their **percentile**, **category**, **branch**, and **college preferences**.

Trusted by **700+ students**, it aggregates over **226,000+ verified cutoff records** from the official State CET Cell PDFs directly into a seamless, searchable interface.

## 🔍 Features

- 🎯 **Massive Database:** Search across 226K+ official cutoff records.
- 📊 **Granular Filtering:** Narrow down by **percentile**, **branch**, **category**, or specific **college name**.
- 🧾 **Ranked Results:** View your eligible colleges neatly sorted by closing percentile in a paginated table.
- 📥 **Export to PDF:** Download your eligible college list to share with mentors or parents.
- 💡 **Fully Client-Side:** Built using **Vanilla JS** and powered by **PapaParse** for instant CSV data filtering right in the browser (zero backend latency).
- 📈 **SEO Optimized:** Full Open Graph metadata, Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, and fully crawlable semantics.

## 💡 How It Works

1. **Input Details:**
   - Your **MHT-CET Percentile**
   - Choose Academic Year: **2025-26**, **2024-25**, or **2023-24**
   - Choose CAP Round: **1 to 4**
   - (Optional) Refine by **Category**, **Branch**, or **College**
     
2. **Results Display:**
   - A clean list of eligible colleges whose cutoff is below or equal to your percentile.
   - Includes: **College Name, Branch, Category, Percentile, and Rank**.
   - Results are automatically paginated.
   - Click “Download Results” to save a PDF copy.
