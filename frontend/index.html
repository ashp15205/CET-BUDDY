About

MHT CET College Predictor 2026 – CET Buddy

CET Buddy is built to make Maharashtra engineering admissions easier to understand. It brings official CAP Round cutoff data — across all rounds and the last 3 academic years — into one focused experience so students can quickly check likely colleges for B.E. and B.Tech programs.

Instead of scanning multiple PDFs and cutoff tables manually, enter your percentile, pick your year and CAP Round, then narrow the list by branch, category, and college preference to get a shortlist that is easier to compare and act on.

Why 700+ students use it

  • No signup or unnecessary setup before exploring colleges.
  • All CAP Rounds (1–4) for 2025-26, and Rounds 1–3 for 2024-25 and 2023-24.
  • 226,000+ verified cutoff records sourced directly from official State CET Cell PDFs.
  • Comfortable to use on phone screens during counselling and comparisons.
  • PDF export helps save a shortlist for later review with family or mentors.
