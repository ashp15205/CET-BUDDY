About

MHT CET College Predictor 2026 - CET Buddy

CET Buddy is built to make Maharashtra engineering admissions easier to understand. It brings official CAP Round 1 cutoff data into one focused experience so students can quickly check likely colleges for B.E. and B.Tech programs.

Instead of scanning multiple PDFs and cutoff tables manually, you can enter your percentile and narrow the list by branch, category, and college preference to get a shortlist that feels easier to compare.

Why students use it

  • No signup or unnecessary setup before exploring colleges.
  • Easy filtering by percentile, branch, category, and college name.
  • Comfortable to use on phone screens during counselling and comparisons.
  • PDF export helps save a shortlist for later review with family or mentors.
